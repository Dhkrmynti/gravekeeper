# Gravekeeper ($GRAVE)

A Halloween memecoin site on Robinhood Chain over the Pons v2 buyback-and-burn engine. The public site is a read-only tracker; `/memex` is the operator panel. Morrow the crow (pronoun: it) stands for the bot. Odd runs are burials, even runs are offerings, the escrow is the hourglass, the dead address is the plot.
