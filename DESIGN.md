# Gravekeeper design

A cemetery at the edge of the fog, one lantern lit. Cold and quiet, where Cauldron was warm and busy.

- **Signature:** the first view is a limestone headstone, with the live burn total carved into it.
- **Inks:** lantern amber = live and actionable, wisp mint = burial, offer rust = offering. Each has one meaning.
- **Type:** IM Fell English for anything carved or titled, Hanken Grotesk for reading, JetBrains Mono for numbers and addresses, Reenie Beanie for Morrow's voice.
- **Shapes:** square-cornered, 3 to 4px, like cut stone and printed programmes. Only the headstone is round.
- **Motion:** the fog drifts, the lantern breathes, the plot deepens as the order of service scrolls. Everything is gated on reduced motion.
